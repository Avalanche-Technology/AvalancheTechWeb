FF1 CO JPR

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how FF1 CO JPR handles information when you use the app.

Effective date: June 1, 2026

FF1 CO JPR is a firefighter skill sheet study and reference app. The app is designed to provide local reference content, skill sheets, PDF materials, and generated study questions without requiring a user account.

Information You Provide

The app does not ask you to create an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, address, contacts, photos, payment information, or other directly submitted personal information.

Diagnostics, Crash Reports, and Performance Data

The app may collect limited diagnostics, crash reports, performance information, app version information, update channel information, device information, operating system information, and basic app interaction events. This information is used to identify errors, monitor reliability, improve performance, and confirm that important screens and app functions are working correctly.

Depending on the platform and service configuration, diagnostic reports may include technical identifiers, IP address, timestamps, logs, stack traces, or similar troubleshooting information.

Study Content and App Activity

Skill sheet browsing, PDF reference use, filters, generated study questions, and other study activity are not connected to a user account. The app does not use this activity to create an advertising profile.

How Information Is Used

Information collected by the app is used to:

  • operate, maintain, and improve the app;
  • diagnose crashes, errors, and performance issues;
  • understand whether app updates and key app screens are functioning correctly;
  • protect the reliability and security of the app.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We do not share information with third parties for third-party advertising. Diagnostic and performance information may be processed by service providers that support app reliability, observability, crash reporting, and app updates.

Third-Party Services

The app uses Expo services for app updates and observability and Sentry for crash reporting, diagnostics, replay, and performance monitoring. These service providers may process technical information on our behalf according to their own privacy and security practices.

Data Retention

Diagnostic and performance information is retained only as long as needed for app maintenance, troubleshooting, analytics, legal, or security purposes, or as required by the third-party service providers used by the app.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Your Choices

You can stop all future collection of information by uninstalling the app from your device. You may also use device-level privacy controls where available.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If the policy changes, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or the privacy practices of FF1 CO JPR, contact the app owner or developer through the support contact listed for this app in App Store Connect.
